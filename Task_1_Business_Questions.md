# Task 1: Framing Business Questions

## The Challenge (Problem Statement)
The objective of this task was to prepare for a meeting with the CEO and CMO of a retail business. The goal was to review the provided dataset and draft four strategic questions for each leader, focusing on what would be most relevant to their specific roles.

---

## My Solution: Strategic Questions

### For the CEO
1. What has been our month-on-month revenue trend over the past year, and are there specific months where we observed significant growth or dips that warrant operational adjustments?
2. Which are the top 10 countries by revenue, and are there specific markets where we see high sales volume but disproportionately low profit margins?
3. What is the percentage contribution of our top-selling products to the total revenue? Are we over-reliant on a small set of 'hero products,' creating a potential supply chain risk?
4. Looking at the correlation between unit price and quantity sold, has our discounting strategy led to a net loss in revenue, or has the increased volume successfully compensated for the lower margins?

### For the CMO
1. What percentage of our total customer base consists of 'Repeat Customers,' and how does their revenue contribution compare to that of newly acquired customers?
2. What is the Average Order Value (AOV) across different segments, and are our marketing campaigns successfully migrating low-spending customers into higher-value tiers?
3. How many unique customers have not made a purchase in the last 90 days, and what specific 'win-back' strategies can we implement to re-engage this segment?
4. Which products are most frequently purchased together (Market Basket Analysis), and can we leverage these insights to improve our cross-selling conversion rates through personalized recommendations?
